GAA in Waterford, Ireland

Waterford is one of the most successful counties in hurling, having won 9 All-Ireland Senior Hurling Championships, most recently in 2017. The Waterford hurling team play their home matches at Walsh Park.

Waterford are also competitive in the All-Ireland Senior Football Championship, reaching the final in 2017. Their most notable achievement in this competition was winning the 1891 All-Ireland Senior Football Championship.
